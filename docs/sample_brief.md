## 1. Disease and market scope
Disease area: Amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS). Central question: how
do companies pursuing ALS treatments compare scientifically and

## 2. Scientific background
ALS is a progressive neurodegenerative disease affecting motor neurons,
leading to loss of voluntary muscle control; most patients die within
2-5 years of diagnosis, typically from respiratory failure. Two
modest-benefit therapies are current standard of care (riluzole,
edaravone/Radicava) — background context, not independently re-verified
in this research pass.

## 3. Company landscape

  specific.
- **Amylyx Pharmaceuticals** (AMLX, direct): Originally an ALS pure-play;

- **BrainStorm Cell Therapeutics** (BCLI, direct): ALS pure-play

- **Clene** (CLNN, direct): ALS-focused (CNM-Au8), also developing the

- **MediciNova** (MNOV, direct): Multi-indication biotech; COMBAT-ALS is
  its lead ALS program.

## 4. Clinical evidence
**Biogen** — VALOR (Phase 1/2/3, reported_mixed): missed its primary
clinical endpoint but showed a neurofilament light chain (NfL) biomarker
reduction, the basis for accelerated approval. ATLAS (Phase 3, confirmatory,
ongoing): no results yet.

**Amylyx** — CENTAUR (Phase 2, reported_positive): met its primary
endpoint; basis for original 2022 approval. PHOENIX (Phase 3, confirmatory,
reported_negative): missed primary and all secondary endpoints, directly
causing the April 2024 market withdrawal. LUMINA (Phase 1, ongoing): early-
stage, no efficacy data expected yet.

**BrainStorm** — Original Phase 3 NurOwn trial (reported_negative): missed
primary endpoint; a post-hoc statistical correction found a secondary
endpoint met, but the FDA still issued a refuse-to-file (Nov 2022) and an
advisory committee voted 17-1 against efficacy (Sept 2023). ENDURANCE
(Phase 3b, not yet started): SPA-agreed design, pending funding.

**Clene** — HEALEY ALS Platform Trial, CNM-Au8 regimen (Phase 2/3,
reported_mixed): primary and secondary endpoints NOT met at 24 weeks; a
separate post-hoc survival analysis (not pre-registered) reported a 49%
decreased risk of death vs. historical controls — a materially weaker
evidence standard than a randomized comparison. This is the basis of
Clene's October 2026 accelerated-approval NDA filing. RESTORE-ALS (Phase 3
confirmatory, planned): not yet started.

**MediciNova** — COMBAT-ALS (Phase 2/3, planned_not_reported): fully
enrolled (234 patients) as of September 2025; topline data expected by end
of 2026.

## 6. Commercial opportunities
Each program addresses a real unmet need, but commercial opportunity
depends heavily on regulatory outcome, addressable population size (e.g.,
Biogen's SOD1-ALS indication is genetically restricted to a small subset
of all ALS patients), and whether any accelerated approval survives its
confirmatory trial — Amylyx's Relyvrio is the clearest cautionary example
of why an approval alone is not a stable commercial outcome.
